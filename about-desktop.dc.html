Still the same table, still the three of us, still testing everything in the ocean first.

How this started

Mum's hoop went green at Bulli Beach. That's the whole reason Aurette exists.

First market stall Wollongong, 30 pairs
People who make it Three — all related
Pieces at launch Seven, and that's on purpose
The family bit

Mum has made jewellery at the same kitchen table in Figtree since the nineties, mostly for friends who asked. We borrowed it constantly. One of us lost a pair at Austinmer and never admitted it. When her favourite hoops came back from a summer of swimming looking dull and sad, she spent a year working out how to make a pair that wouldn't — and we spent that year telling her to sell them.

Why gold, specifically

We live ten minutes from saltwater, so plating was never going to work here. Everything is solid gold or heavy gold fill, and everything gets the same test: one of us wears it for three months straight — surf, sunscreen, gym, shower — and if it's changed colour by the end, it doesn't get made. Two designs haven't survived that. You'll never see them.

We're not trying to be your heirloom. We're trying to be the pair you forget you're wearing.
What made for you means here

Nearly everyone who bought from that first market stall was buying for herself, on a Saturday, with no occasion attached. So we stopped designing gifts. We ask one question before anything gets made — would you put it on at 7am without thinking? — and seven pieces have passed. When the eighth does, we'll add it. Not before.
